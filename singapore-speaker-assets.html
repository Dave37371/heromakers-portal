PROMOTION TIPS

The more you promote, the bigger the room. Here is how to get the most out of your speaker appearance.

📱
Post Early & Often
Start promoting now and post multiple times leading up to July 30. Use the countdown as a hook — 2 weeks, 1 week, tomorrow, today.
🏷️
Tag & Hashtag
Tag Voice to Influence and use #TheSingaporeShow in every post. Tag other speakers to cross-promote and expand reach.
📩
Email Your List
A personal email invite to your list converts far higher than social posts. Use the email swipe copy in your document — personalise the opening line.
💬
SMS & WhatsApp
Personal messages to your top contacts. Short, direct, personal. Use the SMS templates in the swipe file — they work.
🔗
Registration Link
Always include the registration link in every post and email. Every click counts. Link: voicetoinfluence.com/singapore-home
🎥
Record a Quick Video
A 30-second personal invite video performs better than any graphic. Tell your audience why you are speaking and why they should show up.
