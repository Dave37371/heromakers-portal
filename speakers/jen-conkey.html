About Jen

Jen Conkey is a transformational leader, entrepreneur, and mindset strategist on a mission to empower individuals to break free from limitations and achieve extraordinary results. Her vibrant energy — "Jenergy" — has become her signature.

As co-founder of initiatives like the Remote Multifamily Investing Academy, Warriors of Wealth, and WOW Live, Jen has positively impacted thousands of lives by guiding them to unshackle themselves from fear, self-doubt, and societal expectations.

With expertise in NLP, neuroscience, and leadership development, Jen's unique approach combines cutting-edge science with actionable strategies to create lasting change. Together with her wife Stacy, she has built multimillion-dollar companies and a portfolio of cash-flowing assets — and now empowers others to do the same.
