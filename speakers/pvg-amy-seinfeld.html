About Dr. Amy

With over 28 years of medical experience, Dr. Amy Seinfeld is a board-certified physician dedicated to helping patients achieve lasting health, longevity and overall wellness. Her approach combines evidence-based medicine with personalised care, focusing on optimising health from the inside out rather than simply treating symptoms.

Passionate about preventive medicine, healthy aging and metabolic wellness, Dr. Seinfeld works closely with each patient to create individualised treatment plans that support long-term vitality and quality of life. She believes true wellness is built through a combination of medical expertise, lifestyle optimisation and patient education.

Whether guiding patients through wellness programs, peptide therapies, weight management or longevity-focused care, Dr. Seinfeld is committed to helping every patient feel healthier, stronger and more confident at every stage of life.

Her mission is simple: to empower patients to live longer, healthier and more vibrant lives through compassionate, personalised medicine.
