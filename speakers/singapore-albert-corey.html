About Albert

Serving business owners since 1985, Albert Corey brings over 40 years of accounting, tax and business consulting experience. He has helped 45,000+ business owners increase profits, improve cash flow and reduce taxes.

Albert is a four-time Amazon bestselling author. His latest book, Unlock the Profit Path, reveals simple, practical ways business owners can increase profits, improve cash flow and build a stronger financial future. He has worked alongside and shared the stage with renowned business leaders including Tony Robbins, Grant Cardone and Bill Walsh.

Albert is the creator of the CFO in a Box™ system, helping entrepreneurs stop guessing where their money goes and start building lasting profits. At Singapore, he’ll share practical strategies every business owner can use to increase profits, improve cash flow, reduce taxes and build a business that creates financial freedom.
