About Jeff

Jeff Ross is an international speaker, customer value journey (CVJ) strategist, mentor, and digital marketing leader with over 17 years of experience in online business, social selling and influence-based marketing. Known for his ability to clarify chaos, Jeff helps leaders and brands design simple, scalable customer journeys that turn strangers into advocates through trust, story and service.

A master of voice positioning and message clarity, Jeff specializes in helping thought leaders find, refine and monetize their voice through ethical copywriting, storytelling and attraction marketing. As a lifelong student of buying psychology, he understands how people actually make decisions — and teaches leaders how to communicate in a way that resonates with the voice already playing in their audience’s head.

As VP of Field Development at FinancePro, Jeff leads community growth, leadership development and education, helping thousands of families build sustainable online income. He is also the creator of the Hero Makers movement, empowering purpose-driven leaders to rise, build legacy, and serve from a place of identity and integrity. With his signature “caffeinated kangaroo” energy, Jeff blends storytelling, discipleship and business strategy to help people discover their identity, unlock their voice, and step into a life of freedom, influence and legacy.
