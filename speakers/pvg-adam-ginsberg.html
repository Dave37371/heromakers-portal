About Adam

Adam Ginsberg is a serial entrepreneur, business strategist, AI innovator and health and wellness executive with more than 35 years of experience building companies, developing high-growth brands and helping entrepreneurs create lasting success.

As founder and CEO of Internet Mastery, Adam built one of the world’s most recognised ecommerce education companies, training entrepreneurs across more than 25 countries in Amazon, ecommerce, artificial intelligence, sales, marketing and business growth. Internet Mastery students have generated hundreds of millions of dollars in ecommerce sales. He is a six-time bestselling author and has shared the stage with Tony Robbins, Robert Kiyosaki, Les Brown and Daymond John.

Today Adam serves as CEO and co-founder of Ginspire Health and DrSeinfeld.com, combining technology with physician-guided wellness. Working alongside Dr. Amy Seinfeld, he has helped build one of the fastest-growing brands focused on peptide wellness, longevity, cognitive performance, hormone optimisation and healthy aging.

Known for making complex subjects easy to understand, Adam delivers high-energy presentations that blend real-world business experience with strategies audiences can implement immediately.
