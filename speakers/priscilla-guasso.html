About Priscilla

Priscilla Guasso is a powerhouse HR executive, certified executive coach, and CEO of Manifesting Leadership Group, where she serves as a fractional HR executive delivering executive-level people strategy to scaling founders without the full-time overhead.

With over 20 years of experience and clients across corporate, nonprofit, healthcare, and hospitality sectors, she partners with visionary leaders to build scalable people infrastructure, strengthen culture, and align leadership with measurable growth. She is a 4x published and 2x Amazon Best Selling author who transformed a pandemic vision, Latinas Rising Up In HR, into a thriving global leadership movement impacting more than 11,000 women across the United States and Latin America.

Priscilla blends strategy and soul, elevating leadership capacity and creating spaces where women do not just network… they rise. Chicago born and Miami based, wife and mother, she speaks from lived experience — rising through corporate executive leadership while navigating infertility, reinvention, entrepreneurship, and executive influence.
