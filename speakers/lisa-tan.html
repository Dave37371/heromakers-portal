About Lisa

Dr Lisa Tan is the Director of Global Alliance for Pásalo Pa’lante (Pass Kindness Forward), a worldwide movement working to inspire one billion acts of kindness and strengthen human connection across 195+ countries.

At a time when humanity is more digitally connected than ever yet increasingly disconnected from one another, she helps lead a global alliance of educators, youth leaders, media organizations, athletes, artists, businesses, and community changemakers committed to measurable positive impact through kindness in action. Pásalo Pa’lante is collaborating with the HeartMath Institute to explore how collective acts of kindness and conscious action contribute to greater global coherence and connection.

Her work focuses on building bridges across cultures, generations, and communities — uniting humanity beyond politics, religion, and borders. Kindness in Action. Change the world.
