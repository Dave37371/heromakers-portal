About Dr. Joseph

Dr. Joseph McClendon III is a globally recognised performance scientist, neuropsychologist and one of the world’s leading authorities on human potential and personal transformation. For over four decades he has helped elite performers rewire their minds to achieve peak performance under pressure.

He is the creator of Neuroencoding®, a brain-based system designed to program the mind and nervous system to default to excellence — regardless of environment, circumstance or challenge. Neuroencoding integrates neuroscience, psychology, physiology and behavioural conditioning to create rapid, lasting transformation.

Dr. McClendon has spoken live to over six million people worldwide and regularly coaches audiences of 10,000 or more at a time. He has been invited to teach at prestigious institutions including UCLA, where his work has influenced leadership, performance and human behaviour programs.

For more than 30 years he served as one of Tony Robbins’ closest collaborators and lead trainers, helping train Robbins’ global coaching teams and working behind the scenes on some of the most influential personal development programs in the world. His methodologies have been trusted by Olympic athletes, Grammy winners, Academy Award winners, Fortune 500 executives and billionaire entrepreneurs.

As founder of the Neuroencoding Institute, Dr. McClendon certifies coaches and leaders around the world, equipping them with tools to help people go further and faster — mentally, emotionally and physically. His life’s work centres on one mission: helping people transcend limitations, reclaim personal power and live from their highest potential, not through motivation alone but through genuine neurological transformation.
