About Carmen

Carmen Skipworth is a global AI and technology strategist, international author and speaker focused on helping leaders navigate the transformative power of artificial intelligence. She is the author of Lead with Intelligence: A Woman’s Guide to Thriving in the Age of AI and host of the podcast AI Made Simple with Carmen, where she turns complex AI topics into practical strategies for leaders and organisations.

Carmen contributes to the Forbes Business Council, writing on AI, leadership and the future of work. She has spoken at international forums including the United Nations on ethical AI and responsible innovation. Her work advancing women in leadership and technology has been featured in Formidable Woman Magazine.

With a background in technology, digital transformation and national security space initiatives, Carmen helps executives and emerging leaders move beyond experimenting with AI and start leading strategic, intelligent transformation.
