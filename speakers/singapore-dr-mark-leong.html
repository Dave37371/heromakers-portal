About Dr. Mark

Dr. Mark Leong is an Award-Winning Global Healthcare Leader, Entrepreneur, Bestselling Author, and Founder & CEO of Farmz Asia. With over 1 million social media followers and one of Asia’s fastest-growing wellness communities, he has dedicated the past decade to helping people take control of their health while exposing the hidden truths behind food, supplements and modern healthcare.

A two-time Singapore Book of Records holder, Dr. Mark holds records for the Biggest Weight Loss in 7 Days and the Biggest Community Health Event. Through his signature Revival Method, he has helped thousands of people across Asia improve their health, manage their weight and transform their lives through preventive wellness, nutrition and lifestyle interventions.

Dr. Mark is also the creator and host of Asia’s leading health education platform, The MLC Show, where he regularly uncovers misleading food labels, exposes health myths, reviews supplement claims and brings together scientists, doctors and industry experts to empower the public with evidence-based health knowledge.
