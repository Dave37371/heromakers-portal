Before You Post

Five Things That Actually Help

Production is handled. Getting people in the room is where you make the difference.

01Post more than once. One announcement reaches a fraction of your audience. Three posts across three weeks is the minimum that moves numbers.
02Always link the registration page, never the watch page. Unregistered people can't be reminded, and reminders drive show-up rate.
03Say why you said yes. A personal line about what you're speaking on outperforms any polished graphic on its own.
04Tag Voice to Influence and PodGo where the platform allows it.
05Send a direct message to ten people who'd genuinely benefit. Consistently outperforms broadcast posting, and it takes ten minutes.
