Before You Post

Five Things That Actually Help

Attendance is the whole game. Production is handled — getting people in the room is where you make the difference.

01 Post more than once. One announcement gets seen by a fraction of your audience. Three posts across three weeks is the minimum that moves numbers.
02 Always link the registration page, never the watch page. Unregistered people can't be reminded, and reminders are what drive show-up rate.
03 Say why you said yes. A personal line about what you're speaking on outperforms any polished graphic on its own.
04 Tag Voice to Influence and PodGo where the platform allows it.
05 Send a direct message to ten people who'd genuinely benefit. This consistently outperforms broadcast posting, and it takes ten minutes.
