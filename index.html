About Joseph
Joseph Bograd is a seasoned real estate investor and entrepreneur who has built a multi-million dollar portfolio across residential, commercial and short-term rental markets. With over a decade of experience navigating market cycles, Joseph brings a ground-level perspective that combines data-driven strategy with the creative thinking of a true entrepreneur.
He is known for his ability to identify undervalued markets before they peak, and for teaching others to do the same — without the fluff that dominates most real estate education. His approach is direct, evidence-based and immediately actionable.
